p1 · main line · accepted

No commercially available autonomous seeder can plant a quarter-acre plot for less than ten times the cost of hired labor in its first season. The cheapest garden-scale autonomous seeder sells for $7,995. That seeder's maximum footprint is 3 by 6 meters, 18 square meters. Field-scale autonomous seeders cost about $60,000 and are designed for fields of 20 to 30 hectares. Hired crop labor costs $17.15 per hour at the US median. A walk-behind push seeder plants a full 30-inch bed in one or two walking passes.
